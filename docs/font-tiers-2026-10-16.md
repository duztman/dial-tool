## What the tiers mean (Okay, 16 Oct: "tiers, top ~20 in the picker")

- **Top:** the picker's list. Chosen by voice, to add what the shipped fonts lack, one or two fonts per voice.
- **Middle:** accepted and held back. First in line if a top slot opens or the picker grows.
- **Bottom:** accepted, not in line: a near-twin of a top or shipped font (90% or more alike, measured), a file that needs subsetting first, or a specialist face.

"Alike" is the measured shape match (digits, capitals, lowercase; 100% = the same drawing). In the last column it is the closest of the shipped families. Where a middle or bottom font is a near-twin of a top one, the reason says so.
