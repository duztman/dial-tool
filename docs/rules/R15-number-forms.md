# R15 · A number has three ways in — type it, step it, sweep it

**A number has three ways in — type it, step it, sweep it** kept in sync, the same in every form. Typing past the slider's range extends it. A row's **form** says which of the three it shows; the others are one press away, in the number's popup. The forms are listed here, so a new form is a new line in this list, not an exception (Okay, 16 Oct 2026: "r15 sounds good"):
  - **A · Full row**: label · slider · typed field · stepper. The starting form, and the one for numbers explored by sweeping (radius, size, length). **One number box** in every form (Okay, 18 Oct 2026, 21:20: "This version of boxes win … these replace all"): the value's box, then − and + in theirs, all touching, one hairline of the rule's grey; the signs drawn, 12 across in their cell (9 in a narrow box). Beside a hand's detail box, in a quiet row (form B) and in Positions the same box, narrower. Under a finger (Okay, 18 Oct 2026, 22:35) the row's slider and its name open the controller, tapped or slid; the field takes typing and − + step in place, as with a mouse. A **chevron box** (a value chosen from a menu) is the value's box and one ⌄ box, same style — **every menu in the tool is one** (`chevron_box()`; Okay, 20 Oct 2026: "apply universal style to 'arabic like' drop downs"), and a press anywhere on it opens **the tool's own list** (`menu_list()`, the unit box's look), never the browser's ("styled like mm dropdown. all around the site"). In the Guides list the chevron box is round, as high as the list's circles (#539).
  - **Words**: a choice of a few words is a **pill switch** (design canon §6), never a row of boxes; a longer list of names is **free pills**, each a pill of its own, wrapping (`free`; Okay, 24 Oct 2026: "Options can be free living single pills").
  - **Toggle**: on and off without words where a column of them is scanned (Shenanigans): a small track, a travelling dot — off, all black — its outline, the dot at the left, the row's name; on, the name white, the dot white at the right on the black-filled track (`seg` with `bool`; Okay, 24 Oct 2026, 19:04: "On off does not need to be written down. A dot travels … Apple style"). A tick stays a tick everywhere else.
  - **B · Quiet row**: label · the number box and no slider; the slider, the typed field and the rest stand in the popup (a click on the name or the number; on a phone, anywhere on the line). For numbers that are set more than swept: counts, angles, Positions, Nudge, a window's width and height. Declared on a row as `quiet`: a side row whose slider is not shown (Positions\' five numbers, #251; a ring\'s Clearance). Two numbers that belong together (a window\'s width and height) are two full rows linked by a **LINK pill** on the line between them (#358): linked, changing one keeps their ratio.
  - **D · Named slider**: the name over its slider, no box on the row; the value rides in the knob, a pill as wide as its figures (the Shadows pane's Angle · Height · Strength beside the sun picture, #526; Okay, 25 Oct 2026: "numbers on the buttons since we have nowhere else to write"). The name opens the controller. Declared on a row as `named`.
  - **C · Value button**: in a line that sums a thing up (the selected ring's line) a small drawing stands for the number and its value is read beside it; a click opens the number's popup, pressing and moving changes the value .

The popup every form opens is the **controller**: [R15a](R15a-controller.md).
