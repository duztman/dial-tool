# R15a · The controller — one popup for every number

**The controller — one popup for every number** - **The popup — the controller** (one for every form, build list #249; Okay's drawing `controller 2.svg`, 18 Oct 2026, #330): one dark square holding, top to bottom, the value in bold on a grey pill **as wide as its figures, riding over the slider's knob** inside its lane (Okay, 24 Oct 2026, 18:31: "Shrink to the size of the numbers, make it follow the slider button all the way in its lane"), the slider in a white pill, and − and + as two dark discs with white square-ended signs at its foot, one at each end, **the row's name between them, untouchable**; the top pill at 70 % of the slider's pill, the discs at 70 % × 85 % ("plus minus and number line 70% of now … Keep slider big"; "Buttons a bit more smaller %15"), its corners concentric with the discs (their round plus the padding). **Its ground multiplies** with what is under it on a light ground and **screens** on a dark one — dark as the canvas counts it (`main.dark`) or show mode's column — drawn as a twin under it (`.numground`), so the pills and discs stay solid (Okay, 24 Oct 2026: "multiply with the bg at all times instead of opacity"); at three quarters of the drawing's size (Okay, 19 Oct 2026: "Size 75%"). A linked pair's LINK disc takes the name's place; a row's menu (the numerals' anchor) stays on the row. **As wide as the row's controls** — everything right of the row's name, slider and number box — never narrower than the drawing, so on a wide column its slider lies exactly over the row's (Okay, 24 Oct 2026: "spans exactly as wide as the slider below … slider overlapping slider"); opened from a row it stands over that row, its slider on the row's slider; from a control outside a row (a value button), where the finger pressed, the knob under it. Over everything; it comes at once and, 1.2 s after its last use, fades away; a press elsewhere, Escape or Enter put it away at once. A full row's name opens it too; a press on the name moved sideways drags the value (#170); the finger that opens it goes on sliding it. A linked pair's LINK disc stands between − and + (#358). While a number is slid (in the popup, or a name or value button dragged) the guides show as set, even with Guides off; a numeral being nudged hides them (#252).
