# R16 · What can't be used is hidden, not disabled

**What can't be used is hidden, not disabled** rows that don't apply, and a pane whose rows all don't apply goes with them, heading and all (Okay, 19 Oct 2026: "We don't need to see a header we do not have the interaction with"; `sync_rows()` in `panel.js` hides such a pane, so no row or heading needs a condition of its own for it). One exception: a control that can't act just now but is worth knowing is there shows inactive — Undo and Redo with nothing to undo or redo ("Only inactive if unable to do anything but nice to know it's there: redo qualifies").
