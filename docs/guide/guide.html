01

Start

Dial Tool is one Python file that runs inside DrawBot, a free drawing app for macOS. It draws a dial as a list of rings, three hands and a date window, in millimetres.

Install

Three steps, one file.

  1. DrawBot 3.132 or newer, from drawbot.com.
  2. dial.py from github.com/duztman/dial-tool. Branch main is the released version; dev is the newest work.
  3. Open dial.py in DrawBot and press ⌘R. The window opens.
Speed

One optional package.

Knockouts and hollow shapes are cut by combining outlines. DrawBot’s own routine is slow; skia-pathops is 2 to 14 times faster.

In DrawBot: Python → Install Python Packages → skia-pathops → Go. The Export log then starts with “shape combining: skia-pathops”.

Memory

Nothing to save.

Settings are stored as you work. Pressing ⌘R again, or quitting DrawBot, keeps them: the last session reopens.

Export → Save… writes a named copy; Load… opens one. The nine dials in this guide come as such files.

Unit

mm

Every length is in millimetres, drawn at true size. Radii are measured from the dial’s centre.

Angle

0° = 12

Clock angles, growing clockwise. 90° is 3 o’clock, 180° is 6, −90° is 9.

Shape

Fills

Everything exported is a filled outline, never a stroke. A 0.12 mm tick is exactly 0.12 mm in Illustrator.

Order

Top first

The ring list is the stacking order. The top ring is drawn last, in front of the others.

03

Rings

A ring repeats one thing round the centre, at its positions. A dial is a short list of rings, stacked.

{{strip:exploded}}
The idea

One dial, five rings.

The diver on page {{page:diver}}, taken apart. Each ring does one job and the list stacks them. Nothing is special-cased: the triangle is a markers ring with a count of one, the depth rating a numerals ring with one label.

The list{{rings:diver}}
Kind

Four kinds, changeable at any time.

Ticks and markers repeat a shape. Numerals repeat a label. A band is colour between two radii. Every ring carries every setting, so changing a ring’s kind loses nothing.

04

Positions

Where a ring puts things. Six numbers describe almost any track, scale or set of hours.

{{strip:positions}}
Count · Start · Span

A full turn spaces evenly. An arc marks both ends.

{{ui:strip:positions-5:0:count,start,span}}

With Span 360° twelve positions sit 30° apart. With less, the first and last sit on the ends of the arc: Count 10 over 270° is a scale from 0 to 9.

Every · Offset · Skip those instead

Use some of the positions.

{{ui:strip:positions-2:0:every,offset,invert}}

Every 3 keeps the cardinals. Tick Skip those instead and the same setting keeps the other eight. Offset moves the starting point.

Radius

Measured to the outer edge.

For ticks, markers and bands, Radius is the outer end; Length or Band width runs inward from it. For numerals, Radius is the centre of the text.

05

Marks

Ticks and markers share one set of shapes. A markers ring adds a first position that can differ from the rest.

{{strip:shapes}}
Shape

Bar, wedge, dot, or your own file.

{{ui:strip:shapes-7:0:shape,len,w,round_out,round_in,wall}}
Wedge and corners

One number tapers; two round.

Inner width × sets the inner end as a share of the outer: 0 is a triangle, 1 a bar, more than 1 widens inward.

Round outer and Round inner are true fillets, in mm. Hollow wall leaves an outline of that thickness.

12 o’clock

The first position can differ.

A markers ring draws its first position the same, double (with a Gap), as a triangle, or not at all. Scale × sizes it.

06

Give way

How a ring reacts to the rings above it. One mechanism covers every pair of rings.

{{strip:giveway}}
Skip where

Leave out positions that are taken.

The ring drops its own positions wherever the chosen rings above already have a mark: minute ticks that stop for the hour markers, fine ticks that stop for the minute ticks.

Knock out

Cut a gap round what is above.

The ring is cut back by Clearance mm round the shapes above it: markers, markers and numerals, or everything. The date window always clears every ring, by its own Print clearance.

07

Bands

Flat colour between Radius and Radius − Band width: rings, rails, zones and sectors.

{{strip:bands}}
Segments

Positions cut a band into pieces.

Count 1 is a full ring. More positions cut it into segments that tile the span; Fill % is how much of its step each segment covers.

Uses

Rails, zones, sectors.

Railroad rails are two bands 0.12 wide. A band as wide as its radius reaches the centre: with Every 2 that is a sector dial. A short span is a red zone.

Numerals

One label per position: from a built-in set, a count, or a list of your own.

{{strip:labels:0-6}} {{strip:labels:6-8}}
Numbers

Labels that count.

{{ui:strip:labels-6:0:labels,num_from,num_step,num_digits}}
Custom

Your own list, comma-separated.

One entry per position. An empty entry leaves that position empty, which is how two rings can share one circle of hours.

{{strip:placement}}
Placement

Four ways to sit.

Upright, radial, radial with auto-flip, or on path. On path sets each letter on the circle with the font’s own spacing; below 3 and 9 o’clock it flips to read left to right.

Type and nudge

Each numerals ring has its own type, from any font installed on the Mac. Each numeral can then be corrected by eye.

{{strip:type}}
Type

What reaches the outlines.

Family · Styleread from macOS; no fonts folder
Instance · Axesa variable font’s named instances, and a slider per axis
FeaturesOpenType features under the designer’s own names
Languagelocal forms, such as Turkish İ ı
Trackingin 1/1000 em, as in Illustrator
Nudge

Correct one numeral at a time.

Radius ±in or out, mm
Angle ±°round the dial
Rotate °about its own centre
Size ×larger or smaller

Stored per position. Reset this, or Reset all.

Typical

Even the weight.

Two-digit numerals look heavier than single ones. Size × 0.92 on 10, 11 and 12 evens them; Radius −0.2 pulls a wide 12 inward.

The date window uses the type of the top numerals ring.

Hands

Hour, minute and seconds each have their own shape, size and colour. Lengths are millimetres from the pivot, as movements specify them.

{{strip:hands}}
The diver’s minute hand {{ui:dial:diver:minute:shape,len,w,tail,tip,counter,wall}}
Feature and file

Head, ring, disc — or your own drawing.

Arrow and syringe have a head or barrel width; Breguet and lollipop a ring or disc Ø, placed at a % of the length.

A file is drawn pointing up, pivot at the artboard’s centre. Fit file to length scales it to the hand’s Length; Width × stretches it.

11

Date window

A hole in the plate that shows the date disc, with an optional printed frame. Print keeps its distance from it.

{{strip:date}}
The window at 3 {{ui:strip:date-0:date:da_at,da_r,da_w,da_h,da_round,da_frame,da_clear}}
Clearance

Print keeps away from the hole.

Every ring is cut back by the frame plus Print clearance. Markers and numerals leave the date’s position empty altogether, as on a real dial.

Type

The day uses the top numerals ring’s font.

Shows is the day drawn on the disc; Text % of height sizes it. The frame and the day share the Print colour.

Production

The aperture becomes a cut line.

In a production mask the hole is drawn as an outline to cut or drill to.

Classics

Nine dials drawn after well-known types, by eye: approximations, not measured copies. Each page prints the settings that drew the picture.

Presets

Load them.

Each dial is a settings file in docs/guide/presets. Export → Settings → Load… opens it, ready to change.

Reading a page

Rings top first.

Only what differs from a newly added ring is printed; everything else is at its starting value.

Fonts

Named as on macOS.

DIN Alternate, Futura, Helvetica Neue, Bodoni 72. These pages were rendered off-Mac with similar open fonts, so numerals differ slightly.

Files in and out

Vectors at true size, a one-colour mask for production, and your own shapes coming in.

{{strip:output}}
Export {{ui:strip:output-2:dial:out_production,out_mirror,out_live_text,out_dpi,out_seconds,out_fps}}
PDF · SVG · PNG

True size.

Vectors for Illustrator, print, the press, laser: a 30 mm dial opens 30 mm wide. PNG at your dpi, for film: 30 mm at 600 dpi is 709 pixels.

MP4 · GIF

The hands moving.

From the set time, for Clip seconds at Frames per s, in the chosen beat.

Live text

Off by default.

Keeps numerals editable in PDF and SVG. The fonts are then needed, and on-path text becomes one object per letter.

In: SVG · PDF · AI

Your own markers and hands.

Draw pointing up; the artboard’s centre is the anchor. Black is ink, white cuts holes, strokes become outlines. Outline text first; images and gradients are skipped.

A ring is gone

Three places to look

Its checkbox in the list; its Every and Offset; and whether it skips positions that a ring above takes.

Diameter

Nothing else moves

Radii and hand lengths are millimetres from the centre, as on a real movement. Change them ring by ring.

Slow

Check the log

It should say “shape combining: skia-pathops”. If not, install it: page {{page:start}}.

Start over

Defaults

Export → Settings → Defaults. The last session is in ~/Library/Application Support/DialTool.

When something is wrong

Read the log, compare the previews, send both.

  1. Open Export and read the log. At launch it says “preview: canvas” and “all controls connected”.
  2. If the preview looks wrong, switch Canvas to PDF and compare.
  3. Send a screenshot and the log. If the window doesn’t build at all, run tools/dial_probe.py in DrawBot and send its report.
