Each dial is a settings file in docs/guide/presets. Export → Settings → Load… opens it, ready to change.

Only what differs from a newly added ring is printed; everything else is at its starting value.

Proportions were judged against pictures of the type, not measured from a watch. Treat each as a starting point: load it, then move it toward yours.

 Fonts are named as on macOS: DIN Alternate, Futura, Helvetica Neue, Bodoni 72. These pages were drawn with similar open fonts, so numerals differ slightly from what the presets draw on a Mac. Load… replaces the whole dial: rings, hands and date. Save your own first, with Export → Settings → Save… {{classics}}
