Show Case 3D round 102

Paste a dial's link from Dial Tool, or its SVG (Export → SVG: open, drop on the canvas, or paste). Either way the dial comes with each part's ink thickness and each hand's height, as set in Dial Tool (the Inks rows, the Shadows pane's heights).

After Return of the Obra Dinn: two colours, the backdrop as paper, a noise or Bayer pattern, lines over it.

On the canvas, the camera's pair or the sun's says what a drag moves, and each drops a list: up to three cameras, each kept where it was left; the sun, its strength, the room's light or a card. A lock holds one where it is. Camera: one finger turns, pinch or wheel zooms, two fingers (or right-drag) pan; a view puts it back. Sun: the direction is the sun's angle, the middle high, the edge low.

The table takes the Background's colour: a surface is drawn in neutral greys and the colour tints it.

SVG saves the Engineer style as vectors — strokes, traced tones, the dial's marks as shapes and text — whatever style is showing. PNG saves the picture on the canvas at the chosen size; in Engineer it rasterises the vectors. Detail is the trace's resolution. The last file's size shows here after a save.
