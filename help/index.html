The idea

Four ideas the whole tool rests on.

  • A dial is a stack. Date disc, plate with its print, hands, cap: the print is rings drawn on the plate.
  • The ring list is the print, top first. The top ring wins where rings meet; a ring can give way to the rings above it.
  • Choose, then edit. Choose a row in the ring list, or tap a mark on the preview: the panes below edit that ring.
  • Everything is true size in mm. Your dial is kept in this browser only: save a settings file to keep it for good, or share it as a link.

The screen

The window's eight parts, by number.

①Title line
The mark opens About; the version; Help, this page, in a new tab; the Gallery; the unit box: mm, pt, in, lig, thou.
②Tabs
Dial · Rings · Hands · Windows · Export. Each opens its panes; pinned panes stay in view while the rest scrolls.
③Preview
The dial at true proportions. Scroll zooms, right-drag moves, two fingers pinch and move.
④Top left
Undo · redo, the list of steps, and Versions: your saved dials.
⑤Top right
Guides, Case, X-ray: preview helpers, never in a file.
⑥Bottom left
Backdrop, the colour behind the dial; Shadows.
⑦Bottom right
The Tiny preview, the dial alone and show mode.
⑧Bottom bar
Play, the speed, the time and am · pm, the date, Now, the timeline; Zoom out · Zoom in · Fit.

On a phone the panel is a drawer: drag its grip to make it taller or shorter.

A first dial

From a blank face to a file, in six steps.

  1. Dial › Reset › Blank face.
  2. Dial: set the Diameter.
  3. Rings: Add a ring: ticks for the minute track, markers for the hours, numerals if you want figures.
  4. Hands: pick a shape for each hand.
  5. Windows: choose the card at 3 and tick Show for a date.
  6. Export: PDF or SVG at true size; for print, tick Mono print mask under Print.

Dial

The plate: its size, colour and page.

Dial stays in view; the panes under it scroll.

Dial

Plate
The plate's colour: the pill on the Dial heading line, with no row of its own. Navy, #25305A, to start.
Diameter
The plate's diameter. 15–50 mm (typed 5–300; past that it stops and says so), 34 to start.
Scale everything with it
On, changing the diameter scales every measure on the dial in proportion, counted from where the run of changes began, so sliding back lands on the first values. Off to start; kept by this window, not carried in a link.
Weight %
Every width on the dial at once: marks, bands, hands, frames. Lengths and places stay; one undo takes it back. 50–200 %, 100 to start; kept by this window, not carried in a link.
Centre hole
The hole in the plate for the hands' pipes, as Ø; 0 for none. 0–6 mm, 2.7 to start. The pipes fill it, so it shows only while the hands are hidden; in a mask its outline is a cut line (Print › Cut lines), and the spec sheet lists it.
Background
A note, no button: your own picture, from an SVG file, comes after v1.

Page

Page margin
Room round the dial on the exported page, and for registration marks; the page is Diameter + 2 × margin. 0–10 mm, 3 to start.
Backdrop
The colour behind the dial in the preview only, never in a file; the same colour as the Backdrop switch at the preview's bottom left.

Colours

Colours
Every colour on the dial in one list: a line per ring, top first, by its name or kind (a band with more than one position: a swatch per sector); Hour hand, Minute hand, Seconds hand; Cap; Window 1–4 for each window that is on, its frame colour. Folded, its heading line shows the dial's colours as tiny swatches.

Reset

Reset to the First dial
The button on the Reset heading line: replaces everything on this dial (size, rings, hands, windows, colours) with the guide's First dial. Undo brings yours back.
Blank face
A white plate and the hands, no windows, and one ring, hidden: turn its eye on to start. Undo brings yours back.

Rings, top first

One row per ring, top to bottom in the order they stack: the top row is drawn last, in front.

Choosing a row
A press anywhere on a row but its eye chooses it; with the keyboard, Tab to a row and press Enter or Space. The chosen row and its drawer take the colour of the ring's guide.
Eye
Hide this ring · Show this ring; it never changes which ring is chosen.
Grip
Drag to change the order; a line shows where the ring will land. Let go where it started and the row is only chosen.
Kind

Name · Rename
On the chosen row, press the name or the rename box beside it. Enter or a press elsewhere keeps the new name, Escape puts the old one back; an empty name keeps the old one. The box hides when the name fills the row; the name still renames.
Size · radius
The row's two figures: a mark's length (a dot's Ø), a numeral's size or a band's width, then the radius, in the unit box's unit.
The pill
Under the list, which shows 5 rows and then scrolls. Drag it to show more or fewer rings, a whole row at a time; a tap jumps between 5 and all. This browser remembers it.

The ring's drawer

Details
A framed line under the chosen row, in two lines. First, what it draws: a numerals ring's label set and labels; "Text"; a band's Width and Fill; a mark's Shape, then Diameter or Length and Width. Second, where: Positions, and Start, Span, Skip where, Knock out and Edit one numeral when they are set.
Colour
The swatch: opens the colour pop-out.
Size · Radius
Two round value buttons, their numbers in the row above. A press opens the controller; press and drag right or up to raise the value, two screen points a step, Shift ten times finer.

Positions

Every kind.

The picture
Every position as a mark: used ones in ink, skipped ones faint, the first a dot, the span a dotted arc. Drag in its outer band to move Start, inside it to move the span's end, in whole degrees.
Count
How many positions round the span: 1 to 360 from the menu, or Custom… to type any whole number 1–360 (Enter or → takes it, Escape cancels). 4 on Minute ticks, 12 on Numerals and Markers to start; a new ring starts at 12 for markers and numerals, 60 for ticks, 1 for bands and text.
Every
Use one position in every few: 1 to 30 from the menu, or Custom… for 1–60; 3 on Numerals and Markers, 1 on Minute ticks to start; a new ring starts at 1.
Offset
Which of every few is used: with Every 3, Offset 1 takes the 2nd, 5th, 8th…; 0–59, 0 to start.
Skip those
Turns it over: the positions Every and Offset take are left out, the others drawn. On for Markers, off for the others to start; off on a new ring.
Start
Where the first position stands, −180° to 180°, 0° (12 o'clock) to start; 180° for a text ring. A text ring's Start snaps to another ring's position within 2°.
Span
The arc the positions share, 1°–360°, 360° to start.

Press or drag the names Count, Every, Offset, Start and Span to change their values with the controller.

Clearance
The gap a knock-out leaves, 0–2 mm, 0.25 to start. Only on the Knock out tab, and only when its choice is not nothing.

Export a file

Vector · PDF · SVG
The dial at true size as a PDF or an SVG.
Keep numerals as text in the SVG
The SVG keeps numerals as text, not outlines; the font must then be installed where the file is opened. PDF and PNG stay outlines. Off to start; ignored while Mono print mask is on.
Image · PNG
The dial as a picture; the dpi is written into the file, so it opens at true mm.
dpi
The PNG's resolution. 72–2400, 600 to start; the line under it gives the page size at that dpi in px. Past 16384 px a side the PNG is refused, with the dpi to use; past 4096 × 4096 px the log warns: if it is empty in Safari, lower the dpi.

A Wear OS watch face (.apk, 450 × 450 px) to sideload onto your watch, signed with a shared debug key: for sideloading, not for the Play Store.
Its name on the watch
The watch face's name, Dial Tool when empty; it makes the package name too, so several dials can live on one watch. Not carried in a link. Every export writes the thinnest line and the narrowest gap to the Log.

Print

Mono print mask
The dial in one ink, as a mask for etching or printing. Off to start. Where a ring lies on a ring below in another colour, the two merge in one ink; the note under it names them ("Lost in one ink: …"), and so does the log on export. Knock them out of the ring below (Give way to rings above), or export each ink alone.
Inverted
Toner everywhere inside the dial but the marks, for sunk marks. Off to start; shows with the mask.
Cut lines
Each window's and the centre hole's outline, for cutting; off, the mask holds only what is etched. On to start; shows with the mask.
Outline and registration marks
The blank's edge, and a mark in each corner of the page, to lay films on the blank and on each other; with too small a margin, the edge alone (give the page more Page margin under Dial). Off to start; shows with the mask.
Hidden rings still cut
A hidden ring still knocks out and skips the rings under it, so a film made by hiding rings keeps its holes. Off to start.
What the windows show
Off, the windows' holes stay empty: discs, numbers and pictures are left out, for a printer who prints the face alone (hide the hands under Hands). On to start; shows with the mask off and a window on.
Mirrored, for toner transfer
The dial, its mask and the print sheet mirrored, for toner transfer; the spec sheet and the Dial files of A set of files stay plain. Off to start.
Inks · A mask per ink
One mask PDF for each colour the dial is printed in; the button counts them and its tooltip lists their hex. Allow the browser to download several files. Shows with the mask.

A page of copies at true size, to print, mirrored and masked as the ticks above say; beside the button, a small drawing of the page and each copy on it. Print at 100 %, not "fit to page".

Dress
After the Patek Philippe Calatrava 5196, 37 mm, scaled to your dial.

After a 1939 military cushion case, 40 mm.

After the Seiko SPB143, 40.5 mm, with a 60-divot bezel and the Insert.

Emre's prototype, 22 mm, with rails.

Bracelet · Links

Engineer
A technical drawing: every part outlined in ink, each facet in light · body · shade tones by where the light falls, hard shadows.
Miyazaki
No lines: each part one base colour cut by a dark and a light crescent in deeper colours, a flare, and one white outline round the metal only.
Dramatic
A still spotlight on the watch's centre, whatever the sun: the ground keeps its colour out to 45 % of the radius, then darkens to × 0.82 at 70 % and × 0.57 at the rim; over a gradient, black toward the rim. Dramatic also brings the flare.

No spotlight and no flare.
Shadows

How do I…

Start

start from a Gallery dial
①Press Gallery on the title line. ②Press a card, then Edit this dial, or In a new tab to keep yours. ③Change it from the tabs; the browser keeps the session when you leave.
start fresh
①Dial › Reset. ②Reset to the First dial, on the heading line; or Blank face, a white plate and hands, then turn its one hidden ring's eye on. Undo brings yours back.
find my dial again
Versions, top left; or open your settings file or link.
undo several steps
①Undo and Redo at the preview's top left, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z; the tooltip names the step. ②The chevron "The steps, named" lists them round a Now line; press one to go there.
use the keys
①← → move the time a minute, with ⇧ an hour; Space plays or pauses. ②G guides, H hands, X X-ray (while its switch shows), S shadows, E the dial alone or the end of Compare. ③None works while typing in a field, on a slider or a menu; ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z or ⌘Y work everywhere but in a dialog. See Keys and pointer.
type a value
See Keys and pointer.

Rings and type

add a ring
①Rings: on the heading line "Rings, top first", press Add a ring. ②Pick Ticks · Markers · Numerals · Band · Text; the new ring goes above the selected one.
reorder rings
①Drag a row's grip, or press Move up · Move down on the heading line. ②The list is the stacking order: the top row is drawn last, in front.
hide, duplicate, delete or rename a ring
①The row's eye hides or shows it without selecting it. ②Duplicate and Delete on the heading line. ③Click the selected ring's name, or its rename box. ④Its kind icon changes its kind; its settings are kept.
change a ring's font
①Select a numerals or text ring; open Type. ②Press the font's preview or name: the Fonts sheet opens. ③Choose a family; its styles hang under the name, with Size, Tracking, axes and OpenType features below. Add font link (gfonts, url) or Add font file (woff, ttf, otf), or drop font files on the window.
use fonts installed on my computer
①In Fonts, the tab Installed, beside "Dial Tool's fonts"; Chrome and Edge only. ②Allow the browser's question by the address bar. ③An empty list: the icon left of the address bar › Site settings › Fonts › Allow, then reload.
move one numeral
①Select the ring; open Edit one numeral (Edit one mark on a ticks or markers ring). ②Choose it in the Numeral menu, or press the ring on the dial, then press the numeral. ③Radius ± · Angle ± ° · Rotate ° · Scale ×; a numeral also takes its own Font and Colour, a mark its own Shape. ④Reset this numeral takes it all back.
change every colour
Dial › Colours.
lose the 3 beside a date window
It's already gone: markers and numerals leave a window's position empty.
keep marks off a band
Put the band above the marks in the list, then on the marks' ring, Give way to rings above, Knock out, everything above.

Hands and windows

set the hands' shapes and colours
①Hands: All hands holds Show hands and Motion: sweep · quartz · 1/s · 6 beats/s · 8 beats/s. ②Choose Hour · Minute · Seconds; tick Show, Bevel beside it. ③Pick a Shape in the row of thumbs; set Colour, Length, Width, Tail, or drag the detail box's handles. ④Cap Ø and Cap colour sit under Seconds; with no seconds hand, tick Cap on this hand.
make a date window that follows the date
①Windows: press a card, 12 · 3 · 6 · 9, and tick Show. ②Complication › date; Position 3 · 6 · 9 · 12 · 4, or ··· for any time. ③Keep Follows the date ticked and set the day with the bottom bar's day slider. ④Unticked, type the Text in the Font pane.
make an am · pm window
①Press a window card, tick Show, Complication › am · pm; it follows the time shown. ②Shows: Disc · Words; with Words, type the two words. ③With the disc, Jumps snaps it to am at midnight and pm at noon; off, it sweeps round once a day.

Look and light

light the dial with shadows
①Shadows at the preview's bottom left, or S. ②Its arrow opens the pane: drag the sun picture, or set Angle° · Height · Strength %. ③Heights: Cap · Seconds · Minutes · Hours above the Plate, a row for each disc; LIMIT flashes at an end. ④The foot: Shadows · Soft edges · Bevels % · Reset; each hand's Bevel tick turns its bevel on.
see the dial alone
The eye at the bottom right, "The dial alone", or E: the panel slides away.
see the dial at its true size
①Tiny Preview, above the eye: the dial at its own size at 96 px to the inch, an approximation. ②Drag it anywhere; the eye on its top left puts it away.

Save, share, print

save a dial as a file
①Export › Settings file › Save… downloads a .json with every value. ②Load… opens one, or drop a .json on the window.
share a dial as a link
①Export › Share dial as link › Copy link; the box shows its length. ②Fonts travel by name: whoever opens it needs the same fonts.
share several dials as one link
①Save two or more dials under Versions. ②Export › Share dial as link › Versions › Copy set link. ③Whoever opens it sees them in Compare: Add to my Versions · Just look; pressing a dial edits it.
save versions and compare them
①Versions, top left beside undo and redo. ②Save this dial; up to 9 are kept, the next save replacing the oldest. ③Compare: every saved dial side by side; Escape, Compare again or the eye ends it. Sheet: every saved dial on one A4, as an SVG. ④Press a card: Edit this dial · In a new tab · Remove; drag cards to arrange them.
export PNG, SVG or PDF
①Export › Export a file: Vector › PDF, SVG; Image › PNG. ②For PNG set dpi, 72–2400, 600 to start; the line under it gives the pixels. ③Keep numerals as text in the SVG needs the font where the file opens; PDF and PNG stay outlines. MP4 and GIF make a moving picture.
print a mask or sheet for etching
①Export › Print › tick Mono print mask; then Inverted · Cut lines · Outline and registration marks · Hidden rings still cut · Mirrored, for toner transfer. ②PDF or SVG in Export a file, or A mask per ink · N, one PDF per colour. ③Sheet PDF: Paper A4 · Letter · A5, Copies (0 = as many as fit), Room between, A cut line round each. Spec sheet: the dial with its measures, for a supplier. ④A set of files: tick the files, then Export ticked.
put the dial on a Wear OS watch
①Export › Export a file › Wear OS › APK. ②Name it in the field; the line under it reads name · package · signed for sideloading, not for the Play Store.
report a bug
①See Report a bug on this page. ②Add the dial's link: Export › Share dial as link › Copy link.

Words

Anchor
Which circle of a numerals or text ring its radius names, Outer · Middle · Inner (Resize Ring from); that circle holds still when the size changes.
Backdrop
The preview's colour behind the dial; never exported.
Band
A solid ring of colour; two thin bands with ticks between make a railroad track.
Beat · Motion
How the seconds hand moves: sweep · quartz · 1/s · 6 beats/s · 8 beats/s.
Bevel
A hand's two long halves lit and shaded, in the preview only; its strength is Bevels % in Shadows.
Big date
Two square holes, tens and units, each on its own disc.
Blank face
Dial › Reset: a white plate, one hidden ring, no windows; the hands stay.
Cap · Cap on this hand
The disc over the pivot, part of the seconds hand; with no seconds hand, Cap on this hand puts it on the topmost hand.
Clearance
The gap a knockout leaves round the marks above, or the print-free zone round a window.
Compare
Every dial saved under Versions side by side, the panel away.
Complication
What a window shows: date · day · am · pm · moon, or big date.
Counterweight
A disc on a hand's tail.
Dial alone
The panel away, the dial filling the preview: the eye at the bottom right, or E.
Disc
What turns under the plate and shows through a window; its edges show as dashed guides.
Disc hand
A disc on the pivot carrying one marker, cut as a hole or painted as a mark.
Every · Offset · Skip those
Which positions a ring uses: every n-th from the offset; Skip those turns it over.
First dial
The dial the tool opens with when the address and the browser hold none; what Reset restores.
Follows the date
A window showing the date set in the bar's day slider, not typed text.
Gallery
The last pane of Export: dials to open and take apart.
Give way to rings above
Skip where drops a ring's positions under chosen rings' marks; Knock out cuts gaps round them by the clearance.
Guides
Construction lines in the preview, never exported.
Hollow wall
Turns a mark or a hand into an outline of that thickness.
Hub
The round boss at a hand's pivot, Ø per hand; 0 for none.
Ink
In show mode, one exact colour printed on the dial, standing its height in mm proud of the plate, 0.21 mm to start.
Inside the case
A window's tick: its disc must end inside the case; past it the guide shows red.
Installed fonts
The Fonts sheet's Installed tab: the computer's own fonts, in Chrome and Edge.
Kind
What a ring repeats: ticks, markers, numerals, band or text; changed from the row's kind icon, settings kept.
Knock out
A ring cut clear round marks above it.
Link · set link
The address with the dial after #, from Copy link; a set link carries every dial saved under Versions.
Live text · Outlines
Text kept as text in the SVG, needing the font where it opens; otherwise outlines, shapes needing no font.
Marker
An hour mark (index).
Mask
Black on white, for etching or film.
Mirror
Flips the output for toner transfer.
Mono print mask
Export › Print: every ring solid black, no plate, no hands, apertures as outlines (production mask).
Neighbours
A date or day window's tick: the disc's places either side of the shown one, where the hole allows.
Nudge
One position's own correction, from Edit one numeral or Edit one mark: radius, angle, rotation, size, shape, font, colour.
Numerals
A ring of labels at its positions: Arabic, Roman, Eastern Arabic, Chinese, words, custom, or numbers counted from a start.
OpenType feature
A font's switch, such as tnum, onum, ss01 or zero, named as the font names it.
Picture
What a moon window shows: Arch · Phase · Line.
Pies
A band's sectors when its width reaches its radius.
Placement
How numerals sit: upright · radial · auto-flip · on path.
Plate
The dial disc under the print.
Positions
Count, Start ° and Span °: a full turn spaces them evenly, a partial span marks both ends.
Print sheet
Copies of the dial at true size on A4, Letter or A5 (Export › Print › Sheet PDF).
Railroad
Two thin rails along a track with ticks between: two thin bands.
Ring
One entry in the ring list: ticks, markers, numerals, band or text, repeated at its positions.
Round outer · inner
The corner radius at a mark's outer or inner end.
Sector colour
A band sector's own colour, else the ring's; Alternate gives odd and even sectors two colours.
Settings file
A dial kept as a .json with every value, from Export › Settings file.
Shenanigans
Show mode's toggles at the panel's foot: Shadows · Blurs · Gradients · Flare · Bevels · All.
Show mode
The dial in a whole watch, from the rays switch at the bottom right; tabs Dial · Watch · Light · Export.
Spec sheet
The dial with its measures, for a supplier (SVG, A4).
Tail
The part of a hand behind the pivot, in mm.
Taper
A wedge's inner width as a share of its outer (Inner width ×): 0 a triangle, 1 a bar.
Text ring
A line of words at each position in its own font; its Start snaps to other rings' positions within 2°.
Ticks ring
The minute track (chapter ring).
Tiny Preview
A small draggable window showing the dial at its own size, 96 px to the inch.
Tracking
Even letter spacing, in 1/1000 em.
Undo step
The dial before a change, taken once it stands still; ⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z.
Variable axis
A continuous dimension of a variable font, such as weight or width, a slider each.
Versions
Up to nine saved dials, kept in this browser.
Weight %
Dial: every width on the dial at once; not a font's weight.
Window
A hole in the plate showing a disc: date, day, am · pm, moon, big date.
X-ray
Every date, day or am · pm window's disc drawn whole, stacked, each layer fainter; never exported.
