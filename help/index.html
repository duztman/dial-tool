The idea

Four ideas the whole tool rests on.

The ideas

A dial is a stack
Date disc, plate with its print, hands, cap: the print is rings drawn on the plate.
The ring list is the print, top first
The top ring wins where rings meet; a ring can give way to the rings above it.
Choose, then edit
Choose a row in the ring list, or tap a mark on the preview: the panes below edit that ring.
Everything is true size in mm
Your dial is kept in this browser only: save a settings file to keep it for good, or share it as a link.

The screen

The window's eight parts, by number.

The window

①Title line
The mark opens About; the version; Help, this page, in a new tab; the Gallery; the unit box: mm · pt · in · lig · thou.
②Tabs
Dial · Rings · Hands · Windows · Export. Each opens its panes; pinned panes stay in view while the rest scrolls.
③Preview
The dial at true proportions. Scroll zooms, right-drag moves, two fingers pinch and move.
④Top left
Undo · redo, the list of steps, and Versions: your saved dials.
⑤Top right
Guides, Case, X-ray: preview helpers, never in a file.
⑥Bottom left
Backdrop, the colour behind the dial; Shadows.
⑦Bottom right
The Tiny preview, the dial alone and show mode.
⑧Bottom bar
Play, the speed, the time and am · pm, the date, Now, the timeline; Zoom out · Zoom in · Fit.

On a phone the panel is a drawer: drag its grip to make it taller or shorter.

Dial

The plate: its size, colour and page.

Dial stays in view; the panes under it scroll.

Dial

Plate
The plate's colour: the pill on the Dial heading line, with no row of its own. Starts Navy, #25305A.
Diameter
The plate's diameter. Typed 5–300; past that it stops and says so. Range 15–50 mm; starts 34.
Scale everything with it
On, changing the diameter scales every measure on the dial in proportion, counted from where the run of changes began, so sliding back lands on the first values. Kept by this window, not carried in a link. Starts off.
Weight %
Every width on the dial at once: marks, bands, hands, frames. Lengths and places stay; one undo takes it back. Kept by this window, not carried in a link. Range 50–200 %; starts 100.
Centre hole
The hole in the plate for the hands' pipes, as Ø; 0 for none. The pipes fill it, so it shows only while the hands are hidden; in a mask its outline is a cut line (Print › Cut lines), and the spec sheet lists it. Range 0–6 mm; starts 2.7.
Background
A note, no button: your own picture, from an SVG file, comes after v1.

Page

Page margin
Room round the dial on the exported page, and for registration marks; the page is Diameter + 2 × margin. Range 0–10 mm; starts 3.
Backdrop
The colour behind the dial in the preview only, never in a file; the same colour as the Backdrop switch at the preview's bottom left.

Colours

Folded, its heading line shows the dial's colours as tiny swatches.

Colours
Every colour on the dial in one list: a line per ring, top first, by its name or kind (a band with more than one position: a swatch per sector); Hour hand, Minute hand, Seconds hand; Cap; Window 1–4 for each window that is on, its frame colour.

Reset

Reset to the First dial
The button on the Reset heading line: replaces everything on this dial (size, rings, hands, windows, colours) with the guide's First dial. Undo brings yours back.
Blank face
A white plate and the hands, no windows, and one ring, hidden: turn its eye on to start. Undo brings yours back.

Rings, top first

The pinned pane: it stays in view while the panes under it scroll. Folded, it reads "Rings: " and the chosen ring's name, then its kind · colour · radius.

Tools
On the heading line, for the chosen ring:Add a ringMove upMove downDuplicateDelete A tool that can't act is faint: Move up on the top row, Move down on the bottom one, Delete when there is one ring.
Add a ring
Opens the five kinds, each with its picture; the new ring goes in above the chosen one. Escape closes the menu.
A new ring
Named after its kind ("Markers", then "Markers 2"), in the first guide colour no other ring shows. A new numerals or text ring takes the font of the top numerals ring.
Duplicate · Delete
Duplicate puts "name copy" above the chosen ring. A dial keeps at least one ring: Delete on the last one does nothing; hide it instead.
The list
One row per ring, top to bottom in the order they stack: the top row is drawn last, in front.
A row
Eye · grip · kind picture · name · colour (unselected rows only) · size · radius.Show or hideGripRename
Choosing a row
A press anywhere on a row but its eye chooses it; with the keyboard, Tab to a row and press Enter or Space. The chosen row and its drawer take the colour of the ring's guide.
Eye
Hide this ring · Show this ring; it never changes which ring is chosen.
Grip
Drag to change the order; a line shows where the ring will land. Let go where it started and the row is only chosen.
Kind
On the chosen row the kind picture is a button: Ticks · Markers · Numerals · Band · Text, the current one lit. The ring keeps all its settings when its kind changes.
Name · Rename
On the chosen row, press the name or the rename box beside it. Enter or a press elsewhere keeps the new name, Escape puts the old one back; an empty name keeps the old one. The box hides when the name fills the row; the name still renames.
Size · radius
The row's two figures: a mark's length (a dot's Ø), a numeral's size or a band's width, then the radius, in the unit box's unit.
The pill
Under the list, which shows 5 rows and then scrolls. Drag it to show more or fewer rings, a whole row at a time; a tap jumps between 5 and all. This browser remembers it.

The ring's drawer

Details
A framed line under the chosen row, in two lines. First, what it draws: a numerals ring's label set and labels; "Text"; a band's Width and Fill; a mark's Shape, then Diameter or Length and Width. Second, where: Positions, and Start, Span, Skip where, Knock out and Edit one numeral when they are set.
Colour
The swatch: opens the colour pop-out.
Size · Radius
Two round value buttons, their numbers in the row above. A press opens the controller; press and drag right or up to raise the value, two screen points a step, Shift ten times finer.

Give way to rings above

Two tabs beside a picture of what the choice does to this ring's marks.

Skip where
Leaves out this ring's marks under the rings above: nothing · markers above · markers & numerals above · all marks above. "markers & numerals above" counts text rings with the numerals.
Knock out
Cuts this ring back round the rings above: nothing · markers above · markers & numerals above · everything above.
Clearance
The gap a knock-out leaves. Only on the Knock out tab, and only when its choice is not nothing. Range 0–2 mm; starts 0.25.

Export a file

Vector · PDF · SVG
The dial at true size as a PDF or an SVG.
Keep numerals as text in the SVG
The SVG keeps numerals as text, not outlines; the font must then be installed where the file is opened. PDF and PNG stay outlines. Ignored while Mono print mask is on. Starts off.
Image · PNG
The dial as a picture; the dpi is written into the file, so it opens at true mm.
dpi
The PNG's resolution; the line under it gives the page size at that dpi in px. Past 16384 px a side the PNG is refused, with the dpi to use; past 4096 × 4096 px the log warns: if it is empty in Safari, lower the dpi. Range 72–2400; starts 600.

A Wear OS watch face (.apk, 450 × 450 px) to sideload onto your watch, signed with a shared debug key: for sideloading, not for the Play Store.
Its name on the watch
The watch face's name; it makes the package name too, so several dials can live on one watch. Not carried in a link. Starts Dial Tool when empty.

Print

Mono print mask
The dial in one ink, as a mask for etching or printing. Where a ring lies on a ring below in another colour, the two merge in one ink; the note under it names them ("Lost in one ink: …"), and so does the log on export. Knock them out of the ring below (Give way to rings above), or export each ink alone. Starts off.
Inverted
Toner everywhere inside the dial but the marks, for sunk marks. Shows with the mask. Starts off.
Cut lines
Each window's and the centre hole's outline, for cutting; off, the mask holds only what is etched. Shows with the mask. Starts on.
Outline and registration marks
The blank's edge, and a mark in each corner of the page, to lay films on the blank and on each other; with too small a margin, the edge alone (give the page more Page margin under Dial). Shows with the mask. Starts off.
Hidden rings still cut
A hidden ring still knocks out and skips the rings under it, so a film made by hiding rings keeps its holes. Starts off.
What the windows show
Off, the windows' holes stay empty: discs, numbers and pictures are left out, for a printer who prints the face alone (hide the hands under Hands). Shows with the mask off and a window on. Starts on.
Mirrored, for toner transfer
The dial, its mask and the print sheet mirrored, for toner transfer; the spec sheet and the Dial files of A set of files stay plain. Never send a mirrored file to a pad or screen printer. Starts off.
Inks · A mask per ink
One mask PDF for each colour the dial is printed in; the button counts them and its tooltip lists their hex. Allow the browser to download several files. Shows with the mask.
Sheet · Sheet PDF
A page of copies at true size, to print, mirrored and masked as the ticks above say; beside the button, a small drawing of the page and each copy on it. Print at 100 %, not "fit to page".

Scale 0–90 · scale marks · scale ticks · rail · red zone. Shows: every ring on Start −135°, Span 270°; one arrow hand, the hour and seconds hands off, with Cap on this hand.
Bauhaus

Field, Diver, Sector, Gauge and Bauhaus are five of the guidebook's classics; their note says so. Sector is Okay's own version.

How do I…

Start

start from a Gallery dial

  1. Press Gallery on the title line.
  2. Press a card, then Edit this dial, or In a new tab to keep yours.
  3. Change it from the tabs; the browser keeps the session when you leave.

start fresh

  1. Dial › Reset.
  2. Reset to the First dial, on the heading line; or Blank face, a white plate and hands, then turn its one hidden ring's eye on. Undo brings yours back.

find my dial again

  1. Versions, top left; or open your settings file or link.

undo several steps

  1. Undo and Redo at the preview's top left, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z; the tooltip names the step.
  2. The chevron "The steps, named" lists them round a Now line; press one to go there.

use the keys

  1. ← → move the time a minute, with ⇧ an hour; Space plays or pauses.
  2. G guides, H hands, X X-ray (while its switch shows), S shadows, E the dial alone or the end of Compare.
  3. None works while typing in a field, on a slider or a menu; ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z or ⌘Y work everywhere but in a dialog. See Keys and pointer.

type a value

  1. See Keys and pointer.

Rings and type

add a ring

  1. Rings: on the heading line "Rings, top first", press Add a ring.
  2. Pick Ticks · Markers · Numerals · Band · Text; the new ring goes above the selected one.

reorder rings

  1. Drag a row's grip, or press Move up · Move down on the heading line.
  2. The list is the stacking order: the top row is drawn last, in front.

hide, duplicate, delete or rename a ring

  1. The row's eye hides or shows it without selecting it.
  2. Duplicate and Delete on the heading line.
  3. Click the selected ring's name, or its rename box.
  4. Its kind icon changes its kind; its settings are kept.

change a ring's font

  1. Select a numerals or text ring; open Type.
  2. Press the font's preview or name: the Fonts sheet opens.
  3. Choose a family; its styles hang under the name, with Size, Tracking, axes and OpenType features below. Add font link (gfonts, url) or Add font file (woff, ttf, otf), or drop font files on the window.

use fonts installed on my computer

  1. In Fonts, the tab Installed, beside "Dial Tool's fonts"; Chrome and Edge only.
  2. Allow the browser's question by the address bar.
  3. An empty list: the icon left of the address bar › Site settings › Fonts › Allow, then reload.

move one numeral

  1. Select the ring; open Edit one numeral (Edit one mark on a ticks or markers ring).
  2. Choose it in the Numeral menu, or press the ring on the dial, then press the numeral.
  3. Radius ± · Angle ± ° · Rotate ° · Scale ×; a numeral also takes its own Font and Colour, a mark its own Shape.
  4. Reset this numeral takes it all back.

change every colour

  1. Dial › Colours.

lose the 3 beside a date window

  1. It's already gone: markers and numerals leave a window's position empty.

keep marks off a band

  1. Put the band above the marks in the list, then on the marks' ring, Give way to rings above, Knock out, everything above.

Hands and windows

set the hands' shapes and colours

  1. Hands: All hands holds Show hands and Motion: sweep · quartz · 1/s · 6 beats/s · 8 beats/s.
  2. Choose Hour · Minute · Seconds; tick Show, Bevel beside it.
  3. Pick a Shape in the row of thumbs; set Colour, Length, Width, Tail, or drag the detail box's handles.
  4. Cap Ø and Cap colour sit under Seconds; with no seconds hand, tick Cap on this hand.

make a date window that follows the date

  1. Windows: press a card, 12 · 3 · 6 · 9, and tick Show.
  2. Complication › date; Position 3 · 6 · 9 · 12 · 4, or ··· for any time.
  3. Keep Follows the date ticked and set the day with the bottom bar's day slider.
  4. Unticked, type the Text in the Font pane.

make an am · pm window

  1. Press a window card, tick Show, Complication › am · pm; it follows the time shown.
  2. Shows: Disc · Words; with Words, type the two words.
  3. With the disc, Jumps snaps it to am at midnight and pm at noon; off, it sweeps round once a day.

Look and light

light the dial with shadows

  1. Shadows at the preview's bottom left, or S.
  2. Its arrow opens the pane: drag the sun picture, or set Angle° · Height · Strength %.
  3. Heights: Cap · Seconds · Minutes · Hours above the Plate, a row for each disc; LIMIT flashes at an end.
  4. The foot: Shadows · Soft edges · Bevels % · Reset; each hand's Bevel tick turns its bevel on.

see the dial alone

  1. The eye at the bottom right, "The dial alone", or E: the panel slides away.

see the dial at its true size

  1. Tiny Preview, above the eye: the dial at its own size at 96 px to the inch, an approximation.
  2. Drag it anywhere; the eye on its top left puts it away.

Save, share, print

save a dial as a file

  1. Export › Settings file › Save… downloads a .json with every value.
  2. Load… opens one, or drop a .json on the window.

share a dial as a link

  1. Export › Share dial as link › Copy link; the box shows its length.
  2. Fonts travel by name: whoever opens it needs the same fonts.

share several dials as one link

  1. Save two or more dials under Versions.
  2. Export › Share dial as link › Versions › Copy set link.
  3. Whoever opens it sees them in Compare: Add to my Versions · Just look; pressing a dial edits it.

save versions and compare them

  1. Versions, top left beside undo and redo.
  2. Save this dial; up to 9 are kept, the next save replacing the oldest.
  3. Compare: every saved dial side by side; Escape, Compare again or the eye ends it. Sheet: every saved dial on one A4, as an SVG.
  4. Press a card: Edit this dial · In a new tab · Remove; drag cards to arrange them.

export PNG, SVG or PDF

  1. Export › Export a file: Vector › PDF, SVG; Image › PNG.
  2. For PNG set dpi, 72–2400, 600 to start; the line under it gives the pixels.
  3. Keep numerals as text in the SVG needs the font where the file opens; PDF and PNG stay outlines. MP4 and GIF make a moving picture.

print a mask or sheet for etching

  1. Export › Print › tick Mono print mask; then Inverted · Cut lines · Outline and registration marks · Hidden rings still cut · Mirrored, for toner transfer.
  2. PDF or SVG in Export a file, or A mask per ink · N, one PDF per colour.
  3. Sheet PDF: Paper A4 · Letter · A5, Copies (0 = as many as fit), Room between, A cut line round each. Spec sheet: the dial with its measures, for a supplier.
  4. A set of files: tick the files, then Export ticked.

put the dial on a Wear OS watch

  1. Export › Export a file › Wear OS › APK.
  2. Name it in the field; the line under it reads name · package · signed for sideloading, not for the Play Store.

report a bug

  1. See Report a bug on this page.
  2. Add the dial's link: Export › Share dial as link › Copy link.

Show mode

show the dial in a whole watch

  1. The rays switch at the bottom right, then the words Activate showcase.
  2. Tabs Dial · Watch · Light · Export; it opens on Watch: View, Case, Metal, Strap. Light: Style Engineer · Miyazaki, Lighting Dramatic · Flat, Sun.
  3. Export › Export the watch: Image › PNG, Page › PDF, Pixels, the picture's longer side.
  4. The same switch brings you back.

Words

A to Z

Anchor
Which circle of a numerals or text ring its radius names, Outer · Middle · Inner (Resize Ring from); that circle holds still when the size changes.
Backdrop
The preview's colour behind the dial; never exported.
Band
A solid ring of colour; two thin bands with ticks between make a railroad track.
Beat · Motion
How the seconds hand moves: sweep · quartz · 1/s · 6 beats/s · 8 beats/s.
Bevel
A hand's two long halves lit and shaded, in the preview only; its strength is Bevels % in Shadows.
Big date
Two square holes, tens and units, each on its own disc.
Blank face
Dial › Reset: a white plate, one hidden ring, no windows; the hands stay.
Cap · Cap on this hand
The disc over the pivot, part of the seconds hand; with no seconds hand, Cap on this hand puts it on the topmost hand.
Clearance
The gap a knockout leaves round the marks above, or the print-free zone round a window.
Compare
Every dial saved under Versions side by side, the panel away.
Complication
What a window shows: date · day · am · pm · moon, or big date.
Counterweight
A disc on a hand's tail.
Dial alone
The panel away, the dial filling the preview: the eye at the bottom right, or E.
Disc
What turns under the plate and shows through a window; its edges show as dashed guides.
Disc hand
A disc on the pivot carrying one marker, cut as a hole or painted as a mark.
Every · Offset · Skip those
Which positions a ring uses: every n-th from the offset; Skip those turns it over.
First dial
The dial the tool opens with when the address and the browser hold none; what Reset restores.
Follows the date
A window showing the date set in the bar's day slider, not typed text.
Gallery
The last pane of Export: dials to open and take apart.
Give way to rings above
Skip where drops a ring's positions under chosen rings' marks; Knock out cuts gaps round them by the clearance.
Guides
Construction lines in the preview, never exported.
Hollow wall
Turns a mark or a hand into an outline of that thickness.
Hub
The round boss at a hand's pivot, Ø per hand; 0 for none.
Ink
In show mode, one exact colour printed on the dial, standing its height in mm proud of the plate, 0.21 mm to start.
Inside the case
A window's tick: its disc must end inside the case; past it the guide shows red.
Installed fonts
The Fonts sheet's Installed tab: the computer's own fonts, in Chrome and Edge.
Kind
What a ring repeats: ticks, markers, numerals, band or text; changed from the row's kind icon, settings kept.
Knock out
A ring cut clear round marks above it.
Link · set link
The address with the dial after #, from Copy link; a set link carries every dial saved under Versions.
Live text · Outlines
Text kept as text in the SVG, needing the font where it opens; otherwise outlines, shapes needing no font.
Marker
An hour mark (index).
Mask
Black on white, for etching or film.
Mirror
Flips the output for toner transfer.
Mono print mask
Export › Print: every ring solid black, no plate, no hands, apertures as outlines (production mask).
Neighbours
A date or day window's tick: the disc's places either side of the shown one, where the hole allows.
Nudge
One position's own correction, from Edit one numeral or Edit one mark: radius, angle, rotation, size, shape, font, colour.
Numerals
A ring of labels at its positions: Arabic, Roman, Eastern Arabic, Chinese, words, custom, or numbers counted from a start.
OpenType feature
A font's switch, such as tnum, onum, ss01 or zero, named as the font names it.
Picture
What a moon window shows: Arch · Phase · Line.
Pies
A band's sectors when its width reaches its radius.
Placement
How numerals sit: upright · radial · auto-flip · on path.
Plate
The dial disc under the print.
Positions
Count, Start ° and Span °: a full turn spaces them evenly, a partial span marks both ends.
Print sheet
Copies of the dial at true size on A4, Letter or A5 (Export › Print › Sheet PDF).
Railroad
Two thin rails along a track with ticks between: two thin bands.
Ring
One entry in the ring list: ticks, markers, numerals, band or text, repeated at its positions.
Round outer · inner
The corner radius at a mark's outer or inner end.
Sector colour
A band sector's own colour, else the ring's; Alternate gives odd and even sectors two colours.
Settings file
A dial kept as a .json with every value, from Export › Settings file.
Shenanigans
Show mode's toggles at the panel's foot: Shadows · Blurs · Gradients · Flare · Bevels · All.
Show mode
The dial in a whole watch, from the rays switch at the bottom right; tabs Dial · Watch · Light · Export.
Spec sheet
The dial with its measures, for a supplier (SVG, A4).
Tail
The part of a hand behind the pivot, in mm.
Taper
A wedge's inner width as a share of its outer (Inner width ×): 0 a triangle, 1 a bar.
Text ring
A line of words at each position in its own font; its Start snaps to other rings' positions within 2°.
Ticks ring
The minute track (chapter ring).
Tiny Preview
A small draggable window showing the dial at its own size, 96 px to the inch.
Tracking
Even letter spacing, in 1/1000 em.
Undo step
The dial before a change, taken once it stands still; ⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z.
Variable axis
A continuous dimension of a variable font, such as weight or width, a slider each.
Versions
Up to nine saved dials, kept in this browser.
Weight %
Dial: every width on the dial at once; not a font's weight.
Window
A hole in the plate showing a disc: date, day, am · pm, moon, big date.
X-ray
Every date, day or am · pm window's disc drawn whole, stacked, each layer fainter; never exported.
